# Marketing Context

Use this file as the local operating context for content-production and related marketing skills.

## Brand and Offer

- Premium Bali real estate, land, and experience-development projects.
- AILLA is the flagship narrative and should appear as both emotional and rational value.
- Personal brand and business credibility must reinforce each other.

## Channels

- Instagram:
  - Lifestyle lane
  - Professional lane
  - roughly 50/50 over a 2-4 week plan
  - Russian by default
- LinkedIn:
  - English only
  - B2B authority lane
  - Russian master version in Notion
  - English publish version for posting

## Core Audiences

- Developer-investors
- Brokers
- Architects/designers
- Lifestyle expats
- Women seeking a model of family plus ambition

## Voice

- Direct, human, sharp, slightly provocative.
- Never generic AI tone.
- No corporate cliches.
- No unsupported factual claims.

## Editorial Priorities

- Market analysis must use real sources.
- Lifestyle content must still feel intentional and brand-aligned.
- Professional content must be concrete, visual when possible, and decision-useful.
- AILLA, rental yield, market red flags, and partnership logic are recurring themes.

## Output Standard

- One clear audience.
- One clear funnel role.
- One primary platform lane.
- One primary language.
- Notion stays Russian as the working layer.
- LinkedIn outputs should preserve both `ru master` and `en publish version`.
- Facts verified before publication.
