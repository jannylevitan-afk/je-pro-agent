# Je Pro Agent Marketing Context

## Business

- Premium Bali real estate and land.
- AILLA Villa is a cross-cutting flagship narrative, not a separate ad stream.
- Personal brand and business brand are intentionally intertwined.
- The system does not publish automatically. Drafts are prepared for human review and manual publishing.

## Primary Outcomes

- Generate inbound developer, broker, investor, and partner conversations.
- Build AILLA desirability through both emotional and rational content.
- Grow Instagram across two lanes without making it feel like two separate accounts.
- Position LinkedIn as English-language B2B authority for Bali real estate and development.
- Treat the women/lifestyle audience as valuable in itself, not as a hidden conversion funnel.

## Platform Strategy

### Instagram

- Split roughly 50/50 across:
  - Lifestyle lane
  - Professional lane
- Balance is measured across a 2-4 week plan, not by alternating every post.
- The account should feel like a lifestyle-led personal brand with serious business inside it, not a dry business account with lifestyle inserts.
- Default language is Russian.
- English is allowed only for internationally targeted professional posts.
- One asset should use one primary language.

### LinkedIn

- English only.
- B2B only.
- No lifestyle-for-lifestyle content.
- Use expert sources, cases, market analysis, partnership logic, and deal credibility.
- In Notion, LinkedIn items still keep a Russian master version for internal work and review.
- For LinkedIn, store both:
  - `ru master`
  - `en publish version`
- English copy should be adapted for publication, not translated word-for-word by default.

## Audience Priorities

- Developer-investor: ROI, land quality, reliable partners, market timing.
- Broker: partner terms, deal cases, off-market access, Bali expertise.
- Architect/designer: standout projects, collaboration, aesthetics with business logic.
- Lifestyle expat: atmosphere, Bali scenes, community, behind-the-scenes.
- Woman dreamer: business plus family, identity, proof that both can coexist.

## Voice

- Direct, sharp, confident, slightly ironic.
- "Zero bullshit" energy.
- No generic motivational copy.
- No vague corporate language.
- Strong hooks, no cliche openings, no summary endings.
- Personal content should feel lived, not sentimental.
- Professional content should feel expert, not dry.
- Pushback is allowed when a content angle feels generic, unsupported, or off-voice; final editorial choice stays human.

## Writing Rules

- Every post must target one audience portrait clearly.
- Every post must have one funnel role:
  - attention
  - affinity
  - authority
  - conversion
- Notion remains the Russian working layer.
- LinkedIn publishes in English, but the internal master draft stays in Russian.
- Every factual claim must be supported by source material or approved brand facts.
- If a claim is not verified, rewrite it as observation, opinion, or question.

## Priority Expert Topics

- Rental yield
- Rental yield over flipping as the default expert narrative
- Market red flags: front-loaded payments, dumping, overlaunching
- Deal structure and legal nuance
- Developer and broker partnership logic
- AILLA as an experience-development case

## Repurposing Rules

- Extract content atoms from each strong source:
  - quotable claims
  - story moments
  - tactical tips
  - data/stat callouts
  - behind-the-scenes fragments
- Rewrite hooks and CTAs per platform lane.
- Do not cross-post identical copy across platforms.

## Measurement

- Track for decisions, not vanity.
- Key signals:
  - saves
  - shares
  - comments quality
  - DMs
  - broker inquiries
  - developer inquiries
  - calls booked
  - deal influenced
