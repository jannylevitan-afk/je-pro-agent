На зрелых рынках это называется базовым due diligence. На Бали это называется первым разговором — потому что земельный статус, зонирование и права на использование здесь не стандартизированы и часто представлены неточно.

**Final Text:**  
A clean presentation does not close a gap in legal structure.

The most dangerous deals in Bali tend to look exactly right: strong location, credible pricing, polished materials. The risk is not that something appears suspicious. The risk is that everything appears in order — while the legal foundation has not been examined.

Before any conversation about price or design, a serious buyer needs to understand land status, zoning compliance with intended commercial use, lease length and extension mechanics, nominee or corporate structure exposure, permit validity, and who carries liability if actual use does not match the paperwork.

This is not administrative work to schedule after the asset decision. Legal structure is the operating boundary of the asset. It defines what the asset actually is and what you can legally do with it.

A single structural flaw can invalidate the entire investment thesis — regardless of how well the brochure reads.

In established markets, this is called basic due diligence. In Bali, it is called the first conversation — because land status, zoning, and use rights are non-standardized and frequently misrepresented.

A visually attractive deal with vague legal foundations is not a discounted opportunity. It is an undefined liability.
